# Anush: Edible couture for an Armenian bonbon atelier (Concept)

**Type:** concept for a fictional brand · **Industry:** chocolate, gifting · **Languages:** HY / RU / EN · **Built with:** Next.js, React, three.js · **Status:** live demo, sample data only

## The brief
A premium atelier of hand-painted bonbons with Armenian flavours, selling gift boxes, seasonal collections and corporate gifts. The site had to feel nothing like our other showcases and had to be built to sell: more boxes, bigger boxes, more corporate orders.

## What the research said
- Global leaders win on gifting, packaging and story. Their box builders are form-style, not tactile.
- In Armenia, chocolate brands sell occasion boxes, mostly through Instagram. Diaspora gifting is served by florist and hamper sites, not by a chocolate brand.
- Nobody combined an immersive first impression, a box you can actually build, a logo-on-lid corporate flow and a "send to Armenia" flow in one place.

## The idea: edible couture
Cream is the page, cocoa is the ink, and each bonbon's own jewel colour takes over the page as you scroll. A soft, heavy italic serif gives the sensual voice; the Armenian is set in its own serif, never a translation of the Latin look.

## Signature moments
1. **Paint it, then crack it.** The first screen is a glossy 3D bonbon. You paint its shell in jewel colours; "Pour the filling" cracks it into shards and the filling colour floods the page into the collection.
2. **Build Your Box.** Tap or drag bonbons in; they drop with spring physics and squash. Choose 4, 9, 16 or 25 (price in AMD updates; larger boxes show a lower price per bonbon), pick a ribbon, close the lid: it slides down and the bow ties itself, and a handwritten-style card is typed. Share by link, and the recipient opens the box.

## Built to sell (design intent, not measured results)
| Lever | Feature |
|---|---|
| Bigger boxes | Live per-bonbon price falls as the box grows, with a nudge to the next size |
| More boxes | Occasion boxes (New Year, Valentine's, March 8, weddings, birthdays) open straight in the builder |
| Gifting | Yerevan delivery with date, slot, surprise option; "Send to Armenia" with USD/EUR/RUB |
| Corporate | Logo on the lid live, quantity tiers, quote request in a minute |
| Discovery | Flavour finder (5 questions → ready box), pairing guide, 16 bonbon pages |
| Experience | Workshop booking calendar, atelier story told by scroll |
| Trust and search | Allergens and ingredients, FAQ, Product / FAQ structured data, hreflang for 3 languages |

## What is sample data
All prices, dates, stock, delivery fees and ingredient origins. Forms save only to the visitor's browser. The checkout states it takes no payment.

## Honest limits of this version
- Imagery is rendered in code (3D bonbon, SVG cut-open views). Macro photography (cocoa dust, hands painting) is the next upgrade.
- The atelier story scenes are illustrations; real footage or photography would lift them.
- Armenian and Russian copy are drafts pending native review.
